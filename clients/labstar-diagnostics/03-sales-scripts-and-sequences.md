# PHASE 3 — Sales Strategy: Scripts, Sequences & Objection Handling

All scripts assume the caller is a Labstar Diagnostics business development rep ("BDR") calling on behalf of Labstar, a local CLIA-waived lab in Hatfield, PA offering urinalysis (CLINITEK Status+) and urine hCG testing with **same-day results and free local courier pickup**.

---

## 3.1 Initial Phone Script (Cold Call — Independent Practice)

**Goal:** Identify decision-maker, current lab vendor, and book a 10-minute discovery call or in-person drop-in.

> "Hi, this is [Name] with Labstar Diagnostics — we're a local lab right here in Hatfield. I'm reaching out to practices in the area about urinalysis and pregnancy testing turnaround times. Quick question — who handles your lab vendor relationships, you or your office manager?"

*[If transferred/connected to decision-maker]*

> "Thanks for taking a minute. The reason I'm calling — Labstar is a CLIA-waived lab about [X] minutes from your office. We run urinalysis and urine hCG on-site with same-day results, usually back to you within an hour or two of pickup, and we do free courier pickup [daily/twice-daily] on a route that already runs near you.
>
> A lot of practices we work with were waiting until the next day for routine UA results from Quest or Labcorp — which slows down same-day treatment decisions, especially for UTIs.
>
> Would it be worth 10 minutes to show you how the process works and leave you a few sample collection kits, no obligation?"

**Booking the meeting:**
> "Great — I can swing by [this week / Tuesday or Thursday] for about 10 minutes, whatever's easiest between patients. Does [day/time] work, or should I check with [office manager name] for scheduling?"

---

## 3.2 Gatekeeper Script (Front Desk / Receptionist)

**Goal:** Get past the front desk respectfully, identify the real decision-maker without being deflected to voicemail purgatory.

> "Hi, good [morning/afternoon] — this is [Name] with Labstar Diagnostics, a local lab here in Hatfield. I'm not selling anything today — I just need to know who handles lab vendor decisions for the practice, like who you currently use for urinalysis and bloodwork. Is that something the office manager handles, or one of the doctors directly?"

*If pushed to "we're not interested":*
> "Totally understand — I'm not trying to sell over the phone. I just want to drop off some information and sample kits for whoever manages that, so they can take a look when it's convenient. Is [office manager name] usually in on [day]?"

*If asked "what is this regarding":*
> "It's about same-day urinalysis turnaround — we're a local CLIA-waived lab and wanted to introduce ourselves as an option for the practice. Should only take a couple minutes of [office manager]'s time."

**Key principles:**
- Always be warm, brief, and low-pressure — gatekeepers protect decision-makers from pushy callers.
- Ask for a *name*, even if you can't get a transfer: "No problem — could I get the office manager's name so I can address something to them?"
- Offer to drop off physical materials (kits, one-pagers) — this often gets you past "we're not interested" because it's low-commitment for the gatekeeper.

## 3.3 Office Manager Script

**Goal:** Office managers care about administrative burden, billing simplicity, and provider satisfaction. Lead with operational benefits.

> "Hi [Name], thanks for grabbing a minute. I'm with Labstar Diagnostics — we're a local CLIA-waived lab in Hatfield doing urinalysis and urine pregnancy testing for practices in the area.
>
> A few things that tend to matter to office managers specifically:
> - **Same-day results** — usually within 1–2 hours of our courier picking up, so you're not waiting until tomorrow to call patients back about UTIs.
> - **Free courier pickup** on a set schedule — no driving to a drop box, no FedEx/UPS paperwork.
> - **One point of contact** — if there's ever an issue with a result or a missing requisition, you call or text a real person here in Hatfield, not a 1-800 number.
> - **No exclusivity required** — you can keep your existing lab for everything else and just send us urinalysis/hCG, or we can fully replace that piece.
>
> Would it help if I left a few specimen collection kits and a one-page reference sheet so the providers can see how it works? And is there a provider or admin who'd want a quick 10-minute walkthrough of the requisition/reporting process?"

**Close:**
> "What I'd love to do is set up a short trial — send your next 10–15 UA/hCG specimens to us at no cost, and you can compare turnaround and service to what you're used to. Does that sound fair?"

## 3.4 Physician Script (When You Get the Doctor Directly)

**Goal:** Physicians care about clinical accuracy, turnaround time for treatment decisions, and not adding work for staff. Keep it short — physicians have ~90 seconds.

> "Dr. [Name], I'll be quick — I'm with Labstar Diagnostics, a CLIA-waived lab here in Hatfield. We run urinalysis and urine hCG with same-day turnaround — typically 1–2 hours from pickup — using the Siemens CLINITEK Status+ platform with full QC documentation.
>
> The reason I wanted to reach you directly: for UTI patients, same-day results mean you can call in the right antibiotic the same afternoon instead of waiting for next-day results and either guessing empirically or having the patient call back. We also handle the courier pickup so it's zero extra work for your staff.
>
> Would you be open to a no-cost trial on your next batch of UA orders so you can compare turnaround? I can work out logistics with [office manager name]."

## 3.6 Follow-Up Script (After No Response — Call #2/#3)

> "Hi [Name], it's [Your Name] again from Labstar Diagnostics — I left a message last week about same-day urinalysis turnaround for [Practice Name]. I know things get busy, so I wanted to try once more before I stop by with some sample kits. Is now a bad time, or is there a better time this week to grab 5 minutes?"

*If still no response after 3 attempts:* move to the long-term nurture cadence (quarterly email + occasional drop-in) and re-allocate active calling time to other Tier A/B prospects. Re-engage if practice ownership, staff, or lab vendor changes (monitor via LinkedIn/news).

## 3.10 Objection Handling

### "We already use Quest."
> "Totally fair — a lot of practices we work with still use Quest for most of their bloodwork and send-out testing. The conversation we're having with most of them isn't 'replace Quest' — it's about routine urinalysis and pregnancy testing specifically, where same-day local turnaround makes a real difference for same-visit treatment decisions. Quest's UA usually comes back next day; ours comes back same-day, often within an hour or two. Would it make sense to just route UA/hCG to us and keep Quest for everything else? No exclusivity needed — and if it doesn't add value, you can stop anytime."

### "We already use Labcorp."
> "Got it — same situation as Quest, honestly. Labcorp's great for a lot of testing, but for routine UA and pregnancy tests specifically, same-day local turnaround tends to matter more than people realize, especially for UTI treatment decisions. We're not asking you to drop Labcorp — just to try us for UA/hCG so your team can compare turnaround side-by-side. If it's not better, no harm done — you've lost nothing."

### "We do testing in-house."
> "That's great — a lot of practices do dipstick UA in-house for quick screens, which makes sense for basic triage. Where we tend to add value is: (1) microscopy and confirmatory testing when a dipstick is abnormal or inconclusive, (2) QC documentation and proficiency testing support for your CLIA waiver, and (3) backup capacity on busy days when in-house testing backs up. Would it help to have us as a backup/confirmatory partner rather than a full replacement — so your in-house testing handles the easy cases and we catch the rest same-day?"

### "We are not interested."
> "No problem at all, I appreciate you letting me know directly. Before I let you go — would it be alright if I just left a couple of sample kits and a reference sheet with [office manager], in case it's useful down the road? No follow-up calls unless you reach out. And if anything changes with your current lab — pricing, turnaround, contract terms — feel free to keep us in mind, we're right down the road in Hatfield."

*(If truly not interested, mark as Tier C / long-term nurture in CRM — quarterly check-in only.)*

### "We have an exclusive agreement."
> "Understood — exclusivity clauses are pretty common with the bigger labs. A couple of things worth knowing for when that comes up for renewal or review: most exclusivity agreements are tied to *send-out* testing volume commitments, not necessarily point-of-care/CLIA-waived UA specifically — worth double-checking the fine print, since UA is sometimes carved out. Either way, I'd love to stay in touch so that when the contract is up for renewal or renegotiation, Labstar is already a known, vetted local option you can compare against. Would it be alright if I checked back in [X months, ideally tied to contract renewal timing]?"

### "We don't have enough volume."
> "That's actually really common, and honestly it's one of the easiest objections to address — there's no minimum volume requirement with Labstar, and no contract lock-in. Whether you're sending us 5 specimens a week or 50, the courier route runs the same either way since we're already nearby. Even low-volume practices benefit from the same-day turnaround on the occasional urgent UA or pregnancy test — and it costs nothing to set up the account so it's ready when you need it."
