# PHASE 2 — Customer Segmentation

## 2.1 Opportunity Score Formula (1–100)

Each prospect is scored across 6 weighted factors. Re-score after every discovery call as real data replaces estimates.

| Factor | Weight | 0 pts | 50 pts | 100 pts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Distance from Labstar** | 20% | >40 mi | ~20 mi | ≤5 mi |
| **Estimated Specimen Volume** | 25% | <20/mo | 75–150/mo | 300+/mo |
| **Independent Ownership** | 20% | Health-system/hospital owned | Regional group, some autonomy | Solo/small independent group, owner makes lab decisions |
| **Ease of Access** | 10% | Multi-layer corporate procurement | Office manager gatekeeper, but reachable | Owner/physician answers own phone or is reachable in <2 calls |
| **Speed of Decision-Making** | 10% | Committee + legal review (months) | Office manager + physician sign-off (2–4 wks) | Single decision-maker, can switch in days |
| **Revenue Potential (annualized)** | 15% | <$2,000/yr | $5,000–$15,000/yr | $25,000+/yr |

**Composite Score = 0.20×Distance + 0.25×Volume + 0.20×Independence + 0.10×Access + 0.10×Speed + 0.15×Revenue**

## 2.2 Tier Definitions

| Tier | Score Range | Definition | Sales Motion |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | 70–100 | Independent, close, decent volume, fast decision cycle | Direct outreach + in-person visit within 2 weeks |
| **B** | 45–69 | Moderate fit — either further away, system-affiliated with some flexibility, or smaller volume | Phone/email cadence, in-person if response received |
| **C** | 0–44 | Health-system locked, very small volume, or long sales cycle (FQHC, hospital-owned) | Long-term nurture (quarterly touch), monitor for ownership/contract changes |

## 2.4 Tier-Based Resource Allocation (suggested split of weekly effort)

| Tier | % of Call/Visit Time | Approach |
|---|---|---|
| A (Top ~25%) | 60% | Weekly calls, in-person visits within first 2 weeks of contact, sample drop-off kits |
| B (Middle ~40%) | 30% | Bi-weekly call cadence, email/LinkedIn sequence, in-person only after positive response |
| C (Remaining ~35%) | 10% | Quarterly check-in email, monitor for ownership/affiliation changes (these flip to Tier A when a health-system practice is sold to an independent group, etc.) |

## 2.5 Re-Scoring Triggers

Re-score a prospect immediately when any of these are learned during outreach:
- Confirmed existing lab vendor and contract end date → adjust Speed/Access
- Confirmed provider count and visit volume → adjust Volume/Revenue
- Confirmed single decision-maker identified and willing to take a meeting → adjust Access/Speed
- Confirmed independent ownership (vs. assumed health-system) → adjust Independence
