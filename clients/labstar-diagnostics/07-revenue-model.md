## 7.4 Break-Even Analysis

Interpolating between the 1,000 and 2,500 test/month tiers (where net profit crosses from negative to positive):

**Estimated break-even point ≈ 1,800–1,900 tests/month (~85 tests/day on a 22-day working month).**

### Implications

- **Tiers 100–1,000 tests/month operate at a structural loss** under these assumptions — primarily because courier and lab-technician costs are largely **fixed/step costs** that don't scale down proportionally at low volume. This is expected during the launch and early-growth phases (Phase 9, Days 1–90) and should be funded as a customer-acquisition investment, not treated as a failing unit economic.
- **The single biggest lever to improve early-stage economics is route/labor efficiency** — e.g., a single owner-operator or part-time tech handling both lab work and courier duties during the 100–500 test/month range, rather than maintaining separate dedicated roles from day one.
- **Crossing ~1,800–1,900 tests/month is the primary 6–9 month target** coming out of the 90-day plan and into the growth phase — this roughly corresponds to **35–45 active accounts each sending ~10–15 specimens/week**, which is consistent with the Tier A/B prospect counts identified in Phase 1–2.

## 7.5 Sensitivity Notes

- **If net collection rate is higher (e.g., 80% due to favorable payer mix)**, net revenue per specimen rises to $12.00, pulling break-even down to roughly **1,400–1,500 tests/month**.
- **If average billed revenue is higher** (e.g., richer mix of hCG and microscopy reflex testing at $18–20/specimen blended), break-even improves further — this is part of the rationale for prioritizing OB/GYN and urgent care accounts (Phase 1) where hCG/microscopy mix is higher.
- **Model B (telehealth/DTC) revenue is additive** and not included in this table — once launched (Phase 6, Month 6+), even modest DTC volume (50–100 tests/month at $10.50 net each = $525–$1,050/month net) helps offset fixed courier/lab costs without requiring new B2B accounts.

## 7.6 Recommended Financial Tracking (tie into CRM, Phase 8)

Track monthly:
- Total specimens processed (by test type: UA only, UA+microscopy, hCG)
- Net revenue per specimen (actual, from billing data)
- Active accounts sending ≥1 specimen/month, and average specimens/account
- Courier cost as % of net revenue (target: <20% at scale)
- Labor cost as % of net revenue (target: <30% at scale)
- Progress toward 1,800–1,900 test/month break-even target
