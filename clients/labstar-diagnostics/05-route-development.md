# PHASE 5 — Courier Route Development

> **Validation note:** Mileage and drive times below are estimates based on road geography around Hatfield, PA (2321 N. Penn Road, 19440) and typical local-road speeds (25–35 mph on PA secondary roads with traffic lights). Before locking in schedules, have a courier physically drive each route once during the target pickup window to confirm actual times, then adjust.

## 5.1 Route Design Principles

- Routes are organized in **concentric rings** around Hatfield, grouping stops by geographic cluster to minimize backtracking.
- Each route is designed for a **single courier in a personal vehicle or small van**, round-trip starting and ending at Labstar (2321 N. Penn Rd, Hatfield, PA 19440).
- Stops include both **confirmed seed prospects** (Phase 1) and **placeholder capacity** for new accounts won during the 90-day plan — routes are built to absorb growth without redesign.
- Target stop dwell time: **3–5 minutes** per stop (specimen handoff, brief check-in, drop off supplies).

## 5.8 Staffing & Cost Recommendations

| Phase | Couriers Needed | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| **Launch (Months 1–2)** | 1 part-time courier | Routes A + B combined into one AM loop (~30 mi, ~2 hrs); Routes C/D/E run on-demand only as accounts are won |
| **Growth (Months 3–6)** | 2 couriers (1 full-time AM, 1 part-time PM) | Full Route A AM+PM, Route B/C as AM secondary, Route D/E as PM |
| **Scale (Month 6+, 1,000+ tests/mo)** | 2 full-time + 1 part-time relief/backup | All 5 routes run daily; relief driver covers call-offs and overflow/STAT pickups |

### Recommended pickups per day
- **Tier A accounts (AL/SNF, behavioral health, high-volume PCP):** 2x/day (AM + PM)
- **Tier B/C accounts:** 1x/day (AM)
- **STAT/on-demand:** available for any account, dispatched ad hoc (use existing courier's return leg when possible to avoid extra mileage)
